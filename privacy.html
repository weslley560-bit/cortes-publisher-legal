Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy applies to Cortes Publisher, a desktop tool used by the operator to publish short video clips of their own podcast to their own TikTok account through TikTok’s official Content Posting API.

1. Who we are

Cortes Publisher is a private automation tool. It is not a public social network and is not offered to third-party consumers as a hosted service.

2. Data we process

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok may share:

  • basic profile identifiers (open_id, display name) via OAuth;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored only on the operator’s local computer;
  • the video file and caption that the operator chooses to publish.

We do not collect email lists, phone numbers, payment data, or browsing history from end users of TikTok.

3. How data is used

Tokens are used solely to upload videos that the operator already owns. Videos and captions are sent to TikTok’s API to create a post or a draft on the connected account.

4. Storage

Tokens stay on the local machine (tiktok_tokens.json). They are not sold, rented, or sent to advertising networks. You can delete that file at any time to revoke local access; also disconnect the app in TikTok Settings → Security → Apps.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. The only third party that receives content is TikTok, under their terms, when a post is submitted.

6. Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly process children’s data.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the email associated with the TikTok Developer account that registered this app.
